## Q1. State-by-state security deposit rules: caps, segregated or interest-bearing accounts, interest, disclosures, return deadlines, itemization, penalties

### Takeaway
No single national rule exists. A compliant GL module has to be driven by a **per-jurisdiction rules table** with these fields:
- cap (in months of rent, with exceptions)
- holding requirement: none / separate account / in-state bank / interest-bearing / bond option
- interest: none / fixed rate / actual earned / rate published annually by an agency, plus admin-fee retention and payment cadence
- bank-disclosure notice deadline
- return deadline and the event that starts the clock
- itemization and evidence requirements
- penalty multiplier

The strictest regimes are MA, NJ, NY, CT, MD, DC and Chicago. Local ordinances such as Chicago, SF, LA and Seattle override or add to state law.

### Inferences
- **Data model.** Store `jurisdiction_rule` versions with `effective_from` dates. CA's and MD's caps apply by deposit-collection date or lease-signing date, and old leases are grandfathered, so the rule has to be evaluated against the lease date, not today's date.
- **Return-clock trigger.** The event that starts the clock differs by state: vacate (CA, NY), surrender plus forwarding address (TX), surrender plus tenant demand (AZ), termination (MD, DC). Model it as an enum.
- **Interest calculation needs four strategies:**
  1. fixed rate (MN 1%, OH 5% on the excess, MA 5% cap)
  2. actual earned, optionally less an admin fee (NY 1%, PA 1% after 2 years, NJ)
  3. rate published annually by an agency (CT, Chicago, SF, LA, MD's Treasury-linked floor)
  4. landlord election (FL 75% of earned or 5%)
- **Deadlines by jurisdiction:**
  - The shortest return deadlines are NY 14 days, AZ 14 business days, CA 21 days and MN 3 weeks. The app should raise move-out disposition tasks on day 1.
  - Bank-disclosure notices are due within 30 days in MA and NJ, and "in writing" in NY.

## Q2. Commingling prohibitions: when landlords vs. licensed property managers/brokers must keep trust accounts

### Takeaway
There are two separate regimes, and they stack:
- **Landlord-tenant statutes** govern the owner. Many states (CA, TX, AZ, OR, NV, VA, OH) let an owner-landlord commingle deposits.
- **Real estate license law** governs any licensed broker or manager holding *other people's* money: rents, security deposits, owner reserves. It requires a designated trust account, no commingling, fast deposit, per-beneficiary ledgers, monthly reconciliation and multi-year retention. This applies even in states where an owner-landlord could commingle.

### Cited Findings
- **CA DRE (Trust Funds guide RE 13, rev. 1/2014).** Source for this bullet: [CA DRE RE 13 Trust Funds guide](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re13.pdf).
  - Trust funds must go into a trust account no later than **3 business days** after receipt (B&P §10145; Reg. 2832).
  - The account must be in the broker's name "as trustee", at a bank or recognized depository **located in California**.
  - It normally may **not** be interest-bearing. It can be, only at the owner's request and if all B&P §10145(d) conditions are met: named beneficiary, federally insured, kept separate, written disclosure of how interest is calculated, and **no interest benefits the broker**.
  - Withdrawals may be signed only by the broker, a designated broker-officer, an authorized salesperson, or an unlicensed employee **covered by a fidelity bond** at least equal to the maximum funds that employee can access (Reg. 2834).
- **CA commingling rules** (same source, [RE 13](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re13.pdf)):
  - Commingling is grounds for license suspension or revocation (B&P §10176(e)).
  - Safe harbors (Reg. 2835): up to **$200** of broker funds for bank charges; earned fees may stay in the trust account up to **25 days**.
  - Depositing rents and security deposits from **broker-owned** properties into the client trust account is commingling.
  - Paying the broker's personal obligations from the trust account is prohibited, even if repaid.
- **Other states' deposit timing and retention**, from [Hemlane: trust account rules by state](https://hemlane.com/resources/trust-account-rules-by-state/) (updated Sep 2026; secondary source):

  | State | Deposit deadline | Reconciliation | Retention | Rule cited |

- **Landlord-side statutes that impose trust or escrow duties regardless of licensure:** NY GOL §7-103 ("held in trust", no mingling with personal money), MA (separate account protected from creditors), NJ, CT, NH, PA, DC, GA, NC, WA and IA ([NY GOL 7-103](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-103); [MA c.186 §15B](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartII/TitleI/Chapter186/Section15B); [Akerman chart](https://www.akerman.com/a/web/65441/Security-Deposit-Laws-Residential-Lease-State-Comparison-Chart.pdf)).
- **Florida layering:** a landlord's §83.49 deposit choice must be at a FL institution. Brokers holding escrow must also do signed monthly reconciliations under FREC rule 61J2-14.012 ([FL escrow search summary, incl. Florida Realtors](https://floridarealtors.org/law-ethics/library/florida-escrow-laws-rules); [open-exam-prep FL broker guide](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/fl-re-broker/escrow-trust-accounts/reconciliation-records-violations)).

### Inferences
- **Two account modes.** The GL module should model:
  - "owner-held" (owner's own books; segregation rules come from the landlord-tenant statute)
  - "agent-held trust" (broker's trust bank account, with sub-ledgers per owner and per tenant; segregation is mandatory)

  A property's mode should be driven by whether a licensed manager holds the funds.
- **Bank accounts.** Support several bank accounts per company, each typed as operating / trust-operating / trust-security-deposit / tenant-escrow-interest-bearing. Each account carries its bank's state, because CA, MA, NY, NJ, FL, NC and DC all require in-state institutions.
- **Hard blocks, not warnings:**
  - posting broker-owned property receipts into the client trust account
  - paying a management fee out of the trust account more than 25 days after it was earned (the CA rule)

### Gaps
- **Primary rule text not fetched** for TX TREC 22 TAC §535.146, FL 61J2-14.010-.012, AZ R4-28-1103/A.R.S. 32-2151, NV NAC 645, WA RCW 18.85.285/WAC 308-124E, or GA. The timing and retention figures above come from one secondary source (Hemlane) and should be verified.
- **Unclear whether security deposits need their own trust account** apart from the rent/operating trust account in most states. CO requires a separate trust account for deposits per the Akerman chart; AZ and NV are commonly reported to allow pooled trust accounts. Not verified.
- **Not found:** whether an unlicensed owner's in-house staff (not brokers) fall under these rules, beyond the general license-exemption concept.

## Q3. Trust account reconciliation: three-way reconciliation, frequency, retention, audits, negative balances

### Takeaway
The industry standard and the regulator expectation is a **monthly three-way reconciliation**. As of the same cut-off date, three figures must agree:
1. the adjusted bank balance (statement balance ± deposits in transit and outstanding checks)
2. the trust journal / cash book balance
3. the sum of all individual beneficiary ledgers (owner, tenant, security-deposit ledgers)

Regulators also prohibit **negative beneficiary balances**, because a negative ledger means one client's money is funding another's. They treat any shortage, or any unexplained overage, as a violation.

### Cited Findings
- **CA (Reg. 2831.2).** Source for this bullet: [CA DRE RE 13](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re13.pdf).
  - Required: the bank-account record reconciled to the separate beneficiary/transaction records **monthly**, except in months with no activity, with a **record of each reconciliation kept**. The record identifies the bank account, the date, and the beneficiaries and balances.
  - DRE describes reconciling the bank record to the bank statement as recommended, not strictly required by regulation.
  - Unexplained overages may not offset shortages, and they must themselves be reconciled monthly.
- **CA shortage and negative-balance rules** (same source, [RE 13](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re13.pdf)):
  - Aggregate trust liability = the sum of **positive** beneficiary balances. Negative balances are **not** netted.
  - If the bank balance is below the liability, that is a **shortage** violating Reg. 2832.1, unless **every** owner of the funds gave written consent before the disbursement.
  - If the bank balance is above the liability, that is an **overage**, which may be commingling under §10176(e).
  - Prescribed practices: disburse only when it won't create a negative ledger balance; wait for deposited checks to clear before disbursing against them.
- **CA records** ([RE 13](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re13.pdf)):
  - Columnar records (Regs. 2831, 2831.1) or an equivalent system that "readily enables tracing and reconciliation".
  - **3-year retention** (B&P §10148).
  - Records are subject to DRE audit on notice, or without further notice when there is sufficient cause.
- **AZ:** ADRE guidance requires a monthly reconciliation between trust bank statements, client ledgers and trust account ledgers. The ADRE form states that total client/tenant ledger balances must equal the reconciled bank balance ([ADRE Trust Funds Quick Guide](https://azre.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/Trust%20Funds%20Quick%20Guide.pdf); [ADRE reconciliation form](https://azre.gov/sites/default/files/Forms/Auditing/Trust_Account_Reconciliation.pdf)).
- **CO:** the Colorado DRE's reconciliation template (2023-12) requires agreement among the ending bank balance, the reconciliation worksheet, the **security deposit journal total**, and the total of owner and broker liability ledgers ([CO DRE template (via search excerpt)](https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/Reconciliation%20Template%20%282023-12%29.pdf)). The PDF itself returned 403; this is from the search excerpt.
- **FL:** a monthly reconciliation statement, reviewed, **signed and dated by the broker** even if an accountant prepares it. It shows the reconciliation date, the bank statement date, and the institution and account number. It is not filed with FREC but must be available for audit ([open-exam-prep FL](https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/fl-re-broker/escrow-trust-accounts/reconciliation-records-violations); [Florida Realtors escrow library](https://floridarealtors.org/law-ethics/library/florida-escrow-laws-rules)). Secondary sources.
- **NC:** the regulator publishes guidance titled "Reconcile Trust Accounts Monthly" and a case study of multiple trust-account violations ([NCREC bulletin](https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/rec%c2%b7on%c2%b7cile-to-make-peace-adjust-reconcile-trust-accounts-monthly); [NCREC case study](https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/multiple-trust-account-violations-a-regulatory-affairs-division-case-study/)). These were found in search but not fetched.
- **Software and practice.** Three-way reconciliation compares the bank statement, the GL trust cash control account, and the sum of individual client trust liabilities. Misallocation hides in the third layer: a two-way bank-to-book reconciliation can look clean while the sub-ledgers are wrong ([Beancount three-way guide](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/08/property-management-trust-accounting-three-way-reconciliation-guide); [Rentvine](https://www.rentvine.com/blog/how-3-way-reconciliation-works-in-property-management-and-why-its-critical?)). AppFolio's own documentation was not found; its built-in three-way reports are asserted only by third-party blogs ([Steph's Books](https://stephsbooks.com/best-software-property-management-accounting)).

### Inferences
- **Reconciliation feature requirements:**
  - Monthly reconciliation per trust bank account, with a cut-off date.
  - Import or entry of the statement balance, plus a list of outstanding items.
  - Computation of book balance and of the sum of sub-ledgers (positive balances only for the liability figure).
  - Variance flags in three categories: bank ≠ book, book ≠ Σ ledgers, and any negative ledger.
  - Preparer and approver fields with sign-off timestamps (FL requires a broker signature and date).
  - An immutable snapshot and PDF export, retained for at least 5 years. 5 years covers the AZ, FL and IL maximums found; TX is 4, CA/GA/NC/WA are 3, and MA landlord records are 2 years after the tenancy.
- **Disbursement guards:**
  - Block any disbursement that would push a beneficiary ledger negative, unless an explicit override records the written consent of all affected owners (the CA Reg. 2832.1 concept).
  - Optionally block disbursing against uncleared receipts.
- **Deposit sub-ledger.** Keep a "security deposit journal" sub-ledger per tenant as a distinct liability ledger. CO explicitly reconciles it separately.

### Gaps
- **No primary rule text** for NV, TX, GA, WA, or Seattle or Portland property-management trust audits.
- **Not found:** which states require an annual CPA trust audit or an annual filing. NV and some others are reported to require annual reconciliation filings, but this was not verified.
- **Not confirmed:** the official Buildium, Propertyware and Rent Manager documentation on three-way reconciliation; only third-party descriptions were found.

## Q4. Accounting entries for deposits: receipt, liability, interest accrual/payment, move-out application, refunds, unclaimed deposits/escheatment

### Takeaway
A refundable deposit is a **liability, not income**, while held. It becomes income only when it is applied to rent or damages. A deposit that is designated as final-month rent is **advance rent, taxable when received**. Unreturnable balances eventually escheat to the state. Virginia's statute gives the clearest example: 1 year after the 45-day return period.

### Cited Findings
- **IRS Pub 527.** Source for this bullet: [IRS Pub 527](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527).
  - "Don't include a security deposit in your income when you receive it" if you plan to return it.
  - Amounts kept because the tenant breached the lease are income in the year they are kept.
  - "If an amount called a security deposit is to be used as a final payment of rent, it is advance rent," included in income when received.
- **CA:** under CA DRE trust accounting, a receipt combining rent and a deposit is split between the beneficiary ledgers. The DRE sample: received $2,250 = $1,250 rent + $1,000 security deposit ([CA DRE RE 13 sample transactions](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re13.pdf)).
- **Creditor protection:**
  - MA requires the deposit account to be protected from the lessor's creditors ([c.186 §15B(3)(a)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartII/TitleI/Chapter186/Section15B)).
  - In CA and WA, the tenant's claim has priority over the landlord's creditors, including a bankruptcy trustee in WA ([Akerman chart](https://www.akerman.com/a/web/65441/Security-Deposit-Laws-Residential-Lease-State-Comparison-Chart.pdf)).
- **Transfer on sale:**
  - **MA:** the deposit plus accrued interest must transfer to the successor, who must notify the tenant within 45 days. If it isn't transferred, the successor becomes liable ([c.186 §15B(5),(7A)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartII/TitleI/Chapter186/Section15B)).
  - **NY:** the successor is liable for the deposit "plus accrued interest" in certain transfers ([GOL 7-108(2)](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
- **Escheatment:** in Virginia, if the tenant gives no forwarding address, the landlord holds the deposit and may remit it to the State Treasurer as unclaimed property 1 year after the 45-day return period ends ([Akerman chart, citing Va. Code §55.1-1226(B)](https://www.akerman.com/a/web/65441/Security-Deposit-Laws-Residential-Lease-State-Comparison-Chart.pdf)).
- **Interest ownership varies:**
  - IA: earnings in the first 5 years belong to the landlord.
  - NY and PA: the landlord keeps a 1% admin fee.
  - CA brokers: no interest may benefit the broker.

  Sources: [Akerman chart](https://www.akerman.com/a/web/65441/Security-Deposit-Laws-Residential-Lease-State-Comparison-Chart.pdf); [NY GOL 7-103](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-103); [CA DRE RE 13](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re13.pdf).

### Inferences
These are proposed entries for the GL module. They are derived from the sources above, and no source prescribed them in this exact form.

| Event | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Receipt into trust/escrow | Cash – Security Deposit Trust (asset, per bank account) | Security Deposits Held – Tenant X (liability, tenant sub-ledger) |
| Last month's rent or prepaid rent received | Cash | Prepaid Rent / Unearned Rent (liability) — **recognized as taxable income on receipt** (Pub 527) even though GAAP defers it. Keep tax and book treatment separate. |
| Bank interest credited (tenant-owned regimes) | Cash – Deposit Trust | Accrued Deposit Interest Payable – Tenant X |
| Statutory interest accrual where the rate is fixed and paid from landlord funds (e.g. MN 1%, OH 5%) | Interest Expense (owner) | Accrued Deposit Interest Payable – Tenant X |
| Admin fee retained (NY/PA 1%) | Accrued Deposit Interest Payable | Fee Income (owner) |
| Annual interest payment or rent credit | Accrued Deposit Interest Payable | Cash, or Tenant AR |
| Move-out application to unpaid rent or damages | Security Deposits Held – Tenant X | Tenant AR (rent / damage charges) — amounts kept become income (Pub 527) |
| Refund of balance | Security Deposits Held + Interest Payable | Cash – Deposit Trust |
| Uncashed refund / no forwarding address | (Liability stays) → reclass to Unclaimed Property Payable after the state dormancy period | Cash, when remitted to the state |
| Transfer on sale | Security Deposits Held | Cash (wired to buyer) or settlement credit — triggers a tenant notice (MA: 45 days) |

- **Hard rules for the module:**
  - Deposit liabilities must never be closed to income automatically. Closing requires a disposition record: an itemized statement plus the date sent.
  - Itemized statement generation should attach evidence (receipts, estimates, labor hours and rate for WA) and support a sworn attestation (MA).

### Gaps
- **State dormancy periods** for deposit refund checks or unclaimed tenant deposits were not researched, apart from VA's statutory route. Most states treat uncashed vendor or refund checks under their general unclaimed property acts, commonly with 1-5 year dormancy, but this was not verified.
- **No authoritative GAAP guidance** (ASC 842 lessor treatment of deposits) was retrieved.

## Q5. 1099-INT reporting for deposit interest paid to tenants; other tax reporting

### Takeaway
Interest paid to a tenant on a deposit is generally reported on **Form 1099-INT when it totals $10 or more in a year**. That means collecting a W-9 / TIN from the tenant. Only secondary sources were found; IRS instructions were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- **Threshold:** interest paid to the tenant on a security deposit is reported on Form 1099-INT only when it is **$10 or more**. The copy must reach the tenant by **January 31** ([LegalClarity](https://legalclarity.org/when-do-you-need-a-w-9-form-from-a-tenant/)).
- **Why landlords collect W-9s:** landlords, notably in NYC, request a W-9 from tenants to get the TIN needed for 1099 reporting of deposit interest ([Brick Underground](https://www.brickunderground.com/blog/2013/02/rent_coach_can_my_landlord_require_a_w_9_form); [MassLandlords Q&A](https://masslandlords.net/questions-answers-march-2016/)).
- **Bank-run escrow programs:** some banks offer "landlord/tenant lease security" escrow services that handle per-tenant accounts and interest reporting ([Chemung Canal Trust](https://www.chemungcanal.com/business-banking/services/landlordtenant-lease-security-service); [Capital Bank](https://capitalbank.com/business-banking/services/landlordtenant-lease-security-service)).
- **Landlord income side:**
  - Kept deposits and advance rent are taxable income for the landlord (Pub 527).
  - A tenant's TIN is not needed for kept deposits; the landlord reports them on Schedule E ([IRS Pub 527](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527)).
- **Sources disagree on who is taxed** on interest earned in a landlord-titled account. One forum answer says the account holder (the landlord) gets the 1099-INT from the bank ([TurboTax community](https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/taxes/discussion/correct/01/747743)). If the landlord then pays the interest to the tenant, the landlord issues a 1099-INT to the tenant.

### Inferences
- **Module requirements:**
  - Track a per-tenant TIN/W-9 status and calendar-year interest paid.
  - Generate a 1099-INT data file (payer TIN, recipient TIN, Box 1 amount) at year-end for tenants with ≥ $10.
  - Optionally use per-tenant sub-accounts under the tenant's SSN, as bank escrow programs do. That shifts bank reporting to the tenant.

### Gaps
- **IRS Form 1099-INT instructions were not fetched.** Still unconfirmed:
  - the $10 threshold as it applies specifically to landlords
  - the "trade or business" requirement for payer status
  - whether credited-to-rent interest counts as "paid"
  - backup withholding rules
- **State reporting** of deposit interest was not researched.

## Q6. Pet deposits, last month's rent, and other refundable amounts — security deposits under state law?

### Takeaway
In most strict states, **any refundable amount collected to secure performance** counts toward the cap and must be handled as a security deposit. That includes pet deposits, key deposits and often advance rent.

The important exceptions:
- **Separately permitted** advance last-month rent (MA)
- **Non-refundable fees**, which some states allow only if they are designated in writing (AZ, NC pet fee)

### Cited Findings
- **CA (AB 12):** the one-month ceiling includes deposits for pets and keys and advance payment of rent ([CBS Sacramento](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/sacramento/news/california-security-deposit-cap-1-month-rent-ab12-haney-signed-by-newsom); [SF Rent Board](https://www.sf.gov/news/security-deposit-laws-are-changing-july-1-2024); [Brownstein](https://bhfs.com/insights/alerts-articles/2024/ca-security-deposit-limits-effective-7-1)).
- **MD:** for leases on/after 2024-10-01, the one-month total includes any pet deposit. Move-in collections are capped at the deposit plus first month's rent ([Montgomery County DHCA](https://montgomerycountymd.gov/DHCA/housing/landlordtenant/security_deposits.html); [MD DHCD HB693 FAQ](https://dhcd.maryland.gov/TurningTheKey/Documents/HB693-FAQ.pdf)).
- **NY:** the cap applies to any "deposit or advance", so advance rent counts toward the one-month limit ([GOL 7-108(1-a)(a)](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108)).
- **MA:** last month's rent is a separately permitted charge on top of a ≤1-month deposit. It has its own receipt requirements and earns **5% interest** from the start of the tenancy, but not for the last month itself. Combined fees in lieu of a deposit plus the deposit cannot exceed one month ([c.186 §15B(1)(b), 2(a)](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartII/TitleI/Chapter186/Section15B)).
- **Chicago:** interest applies to "deposits and prepaid rent" held more than 6 months ([Baselane](https://www.baselane.com/resources/state-security-deposit-interest-rate-rules)).
- **ND:** a higher cap applies when the tenant has a pet: the greater of $2,500 or 2 months.
- **NC:** a reasonable **nonrefundable** pet fee is allowed in addition to the deposit.
- **AZ:** nonrefundable fees must be designated in writing; anything not so designated is refundable.
- **AL:** the 1-month cap has exceptions for pets, alterations or added risk.

  Sources for ND, NC, AZ and AL: [Akerman chart](https://www.akerman.com/a/web/65441/Security-Deposit-Laws-Residential-Lease-State-Comparison-Chart.pdf).
- **Tax:** a deposit to be used as final rent is advance rent and is income when received ([IRS Pub 527](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527)).

### Inferences
- **Charge-type attributes.** Each charge type (security, pet deposit, key deposit, last month's rent, prepaid rent, move-in fee, pet fee, deposit-alternative fee) should carry:
  - `refundable` (bool)
  - `counts_toward_deposit_cap` (per jurisdiction)
  - `interest_bearing` (per jurisdiction)
  - `tax_treatment` (liability vs. advance-rent income)
- **Lease setup validation:** sum all cap-counted charges and compare the total to the jurisdiction's cap, evaluated at the lease-signing date (MD) or the collection date (CA).
- **Non-refundable fees** should require explicit written designation in the lease document, at least in AZ. Treat any undesignated fee as refundable by default.

### Gaps
- **No 50-state source** was found on whether pet deposits, key deposits and prepaid rent count toward each state's cap. Only CA, MD, NY, MA, ND, NC, AZ and AL are covered above.
- **Not researched:** treatment of deposit-alternative products (surety or deposit insurance, "fee in lieu"), apart from MA's reference to regulations and the bond options in FL, GA, NH, NC and PA.
- **Not researched:** limits on pet fees and rent for assistance animals under federal fair-housing rules.
