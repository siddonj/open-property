# Accounting & Financial-Reporting Compliance for US Affordable / Government-Assisted Multifamily (HUD, LIHTC, HCV, USDA RD)

Research date: 2026-10-29. Scope: what an accounting module must support (or explicitly exclude) to serve affordable/assisted properties. Primary sources preferred; many HUD handbooks are old (4370.2 REV-1 dates to 1992) and the property's **regulatory agreement / HAP contract controls**, so treat handbook rules as defaults, not universal law.

---

## 1. HUD Multifamily (insured / assisted): 4370.2, surplus cash, residual receipts, reserves, AFS via REAC FASS-MF, monthly reports, Section 8 HAP vouchers, HOTMA

### Takeaway
HUD-insured/assisted properties need fund-restricted accounting: segregated accounts (operating, security deposits, reserve for replacement, residual receipts), a year-end **surplus cash computation (Form HUD-93486)** that gates owner distributions, GAAP annual financial statements filed electronically **within 90 days of FYE** (24 CFR 5.801), and a monthly **HUD-52670 voucher** submitted via TRACS that splits each tenant's rent into tenant portion vs. HAP. HOTMA's multifamily compliance date has been pushed to **Jan 1, 2027**.

### Cited Findings
**Handbook 4370.2 & surplus cash / distributions / residual receipts**
- HUD Handbook 4370.2 REV-1 ("Financial Operations and Accounting Procedures for Insured Multifamily Projects") is dated 5/92; its ToC lists section 2-9 "Surplus Cash and Residual Receipts" and 2-10 "Distributions to Owners." — [HUD 4370.2 ToC](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43702tochsgh.pdf)
- Surplus cash is computed on **Form HUD-93486, "Computation of Surplus Cash, Distributions and Residual Receipts"** (handbook Appendix 2). — [HUD doc 35309](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/doc_35309.pdf); [HUD-93486 form](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/Housing/documents/93486_orcf.docx)
- HUD's form instructions state that surplus cash / deficiency / residual receipts requirements "are contained in the applicable regulatory agreement specific to the particular FHA loan" — i.e., rules vary per property. — [HUD-93486 form](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/Housing/documents/93486_orcf.docx)
- Handbook 4350.1 material: the project's Regulatory Agreement or Section 8 HAP contract specifies where residual receipts are deposited. — [HUD 4350.1 REV-1](https://hud.gov/sites/documents/doc_35308.doc)
- Surplus cash (where the regulatory agreement requires) must be deposited into the residual receipts account **no later than 90 days after year-end**, or sooner if the regulatory agreement says so; HUD requires certain projects to include a **Supplemental Schedule of Surplus Cash** in annual financial statements; residual receipts should be held in interest-bearing accounts; **HUD approval is required before any withdrawal**; approved uses include covering real operating deficits, repairs not covered by the reserve for replacements, and certain amenities. — [Withum (2012)](https://www.withum.com/resources/residual-receipts-surplus-cash)

**Annual financial statements (AFS) / REAC FASS-MF**
- 24 CFR 5.801 covers owners of Section 8 project-based properties (excluding Mod Rehab/SRO and PBV) and owners of multifamily projects with HUD assistance or HUD-insured/HUD-held mortgages (Sections 202, 207, 221, 232, 236, 241, etc.). — [24 CFR 5.801 (Cornell LII)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/24/5.801)
- Multifamily owners' AFS due **90 days after FYE**; PHAs and Section 8 contract administrators **60 days**; must be prepared under **GAAP** "as further defined by HUD"; must be submitted **electronically**. PHA audited statements due 9 months after FYE under Single Audit / 2 CFR 200 subpart F. — [24 CFR 5.801](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/24/5.801)
- HUD's FASSUB user guide says AFS data-entry screens include AFS accounts (HUD chart of accounts), basic-statement line items not in the HUD chart, and supplemental data. — [HUD FASSUB Industry User Guide Ch.4](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/PIH/documents/Ch04_Create_New_AFS-Final.pdf)
- A 30-day extension may be requested for extenuating circumstances. — [HUD FASSUB guide Ch.9](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/PIH/documents/Ch09_Admin_Requests-Final.pdf)
- HUD chart-of-accounts revisions (nonprofit and cooperative accounts) date from ~2000. — [HUD doc 26313](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/doc_26313.pdf); [HUD doc 26236](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/doc_26236.pdf)
- A law-firm post claims FASS-MF still validates the Single Audit threshold at $750,000 although the federal threshold rose to $1M (single, unverified non-HUD source). — [Pease Bell](https://www.peasebell.com/?p=1407)
- HUD maintains a REAC multifamily news page with revised due dates (contents not retrieved). — [HUD REAC MF news](https://www.hud.gov/reac/mf-news); [FASS-MF archive](https://www.hud.gov/reac/fass-fha-archive)

**Section 8 HAP voucher (HUD-52670) / TRACS**
- HUD-52670 = "Housing Owner's Certification and Application for Housing Assistance Payments," the form owners use to bill HUD monthly for tenant assistance (OMB 2502-0182, with Parts 1–4 schedules and special-claims forms). — [HUD-52670](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/52670.pdf)
- Vouchers and related TRACS files are due by the **10th of the month preceding the payment month**; payment arrives by the first business day of the payment month; some agencies still require a signed paper 52670. — [SC Housing voucher processing](https://www.schousing.sc.gov/business-and-community-partners/voucher-processing); [MHDC PBCA voucher processing](https://mhdc.com/programs/asset-management/performance-based-contract-administration-pbca/voucher-processing/)
- Vouchers are transmitted electronically monthly through **TRACS**; the HUD **TRACS MAT Guide** is the authoritative file-format spec. — [HUD TRACS](https://www.hud.gov/hud-partners/multifamily-tracs); [HUD MAT guide ch.3 draft 2023](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/Housing/documents/Chapter_3_MAT_TRACS%20Operating_Tips_Post2_Draft_V1_05252023.pdf)
- Signing the voucher certifies each tenant's assistance was computed under HUD rules; false statements carry criminal/civil penalties (18 USC 1001, 1010, 1012; 31 USC 3729, 3802). — [HUD-52670](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/52670.pdf)

**HOTMA timeline (recent rule change)**
- Notice H 2025-03 (May 29, 2025) moved multifamily HOTMA compliance from July 1, 2025 to **Jan 1, 2026**, citing unfinished systems/forms. — [LeadingAge](https://leadingage.org/hud-delays-to-2026-hotma-compliance-for-multifamily-housing/); [Novogradac](https://www.novoco.com/news/hud-further-extends-compliance-date-for-hotma-provisions-to-jan-1-2026)
- HUD bulletin of **Dec 18, 2025** extended the multifamily HOTMA compliance date to **Jan 1, 2027** for all owners in MF project-based rental assistance programs; full compliance mandatory for certifications dated on/after Jan 1, 2027; early compliance possible via TRACS rent override; continue existing TSP/EIV policies until compliant. — [HUD/FHA GovDelivery bulletin](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USHUDFHA/bulletins/4008a60)
- As of the 2025 notice, PIH (public housing / HCV) had not set its own HOTMA compliance date. — [PHADA](https://www.phada.org/Publications/Breaking-News-Administrator/View/ArticleId/18218/HUD-Delays-HOTMA-Compliance-Date-for-Multifamily-Housing)

### Inferences
- **Fund/restricted-cash accounting is non-negotiable** for HUD projects: the ledger must model separate bank accounts / GL sub-accounts for operating cash, tenant security deposits, reserve for replacement, and residual receipts, with withdrawal workflows that record HUD approval.
- **Surplus cash ≠ net income.** The module must produce a point-in-time cash-basis-style schedule (cash less current obligations) per 93486, and block/flag distributions not supported by it. Because terms differ per regulatory agreement, distribution rules should be configurable per property, not hard-coded.
- **Revenue must be split by payer**: gross potential rent → tenant portion (A/R from tenant) vs. HAP/subsidy (A/R from HUD/CA), with monthly HAP voucher reconciliation (billed vs. received, adjustments, special claims).
- **Annual AFS export**: realistically the product does not file to FASS-MF (auditor does); the value is a trial balance mapped to the HUD chart of accounts + supplemental schedules the auditor ingests.
- **TRACS/50059 certification generation is a compliance-software problem, not an accounting one.** Building TRACS MAT-compliant transmission is a large, regulated undertaking (HUD MAT spec versions, CA connectivity) — strong candidate for explicit out-of-scope; integrate/import from a compliance system instead.
- HOTMA changes income/asset calculations (compliance side); accounting impact is indirect (changed tenant rent / HAP split). Effective MF date now Jan 1, 2027 — could move again.

Not verified from primary text: exact definition of surplus cash, distribution frequency (commonly semi-annual/annual), limited-dividend caps (e.g., % of initial equity), reserve-for-replacement deposit/withdrawal rules, and identity-of-interest rules.
- Not verified: the monthly accounting report forms named in the brief (**HUD-93479 "Monthly Report for Establishing Net Income"**, **HUD-93480 "Schedule of Disbursements"**) and when HUD requires them (recalled as required mainly for projects in default/workout, not routinely — unconfirmed).

## 2. LIHTC (IRC §42): owner/HFA reporting, annual certifications, rent & income limits, utility allowances, subsidy split, investor/syndicator reporting, asset management fees

### Takeaway
LIHTC compliance is primarily **unit/tenant compliance** (income limits, gross rent limits incl. utility allowance) reported to the state HFA and policed via IRS Form 8823 — not GAAP accounting. The accounting module touches it through rent charges (rent + UA ≤ max gross rent), tenant vs. subsidy portions, and **investor reporting** (quarterly operating reports, annual audits, asset management fees) whose specifics are set by each partnership agreement.

### Cited Findings
- Owner must determine max gross rent (rent + utilities), then **subtract the applicable utility allowance** to get chargeable rent. — [NJ HMFA copy of IRS 8823 Guide](https://www.nj.gov/dca/hmfa/developers/docs/lihtc/compliance/tc_comp_8823_guide.pdf); [Habitat Group summary](https://www.thehabitatgroup.com/irs-releases-updated-8823-guide-clarifies-utility-allowance-compliance)
- A changed UA must be used to compute gross rents for rents due **90 days after the change**; UAs are usually reviewed when HUD updates area median income. — [IRS 8823 Guide (state copies)](https://www.treasurer.ca.gov/ctcac/compliance/manual/appendix2/Form8823Guide.pdf); [Ohio Housing copy](https://ohiohome.org/compliance/IRS/8823guide_10-09.pdf)
- 2011 revision of the 8823 Guide treats gross-rent overages from computational/procedural errors as noncompliance. — [Habitat Group (2011)](https://www.thehabitatgroup.com/irs-releases-updated-8823-guide-clarifies-utility-allowance-compliance)
- Current version is IRS Publication 5913 (Jan 2024): if rent + new UA exceeds the maximum, the unit is out of compliance until rents are reduced. — [gocodebook Pub 5913 excerpt](https://gocodebook.com/library/us/federal/us-irs-pub5913-form8823-guide-2024-01/d.2.-no-utility-allowance)
- Annual owner certification to the state agency is a required obligation; failure / inaccurate certification is its own 8823 category (11d). — [NAHMA SHCM webinar](https://nahma.org/wp-content/uploads/files/member/Tax%20Credit/SHCM%20Webinar%20November%2009.pdf); [LHC copy of IRS audit technique guide](https://www.lhc.la.gov/hubfs/Document%20Libraries/Housing%20Development/Compliance/HUD%20Reporting%20for%20LIHTC/IRS%20Audit%20Technique%20Guide.pdf)
- Asset management is usually done by the syndicator for investors, from financing close through the **15-year compliance period** or investor exit; reports are typically monthly or quarterly and cover DSCR, occupancy, reserve levels, plus review of audited financials and tax returns. — [TaxCreditAdvisor](https://www.taxcreditadvisor.com/articles/low-income-housing-tax-credit-asset-management)
- Investors pay syndicators an acquisition fee plus an **annual asset management fee**; one Related Capital filing describes partnership management fees of ~1% of equity raised. — [SEC: CharterMac DEFM14A (2003)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001043325/000119312503046122/ddefm14a.htm)
- Example partnership agreement: GP must deliver **audited financial statements by March 1** and **quarterly tax credit compliance reports**; GP forfeits fees if it fails to fund required amounts. — [SEC: WNC Housing Tax Credit Fund VI exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001084075/000101172301500031/wmainlpa.txt)

### Inferences
- **Compliance vs. accounting distinction:** income certification, AMI/rent-limit tables, next-available-unit and 140% rules belong to a compliance module. The accounting module needs only: (a) per-unit max chargeable rent (limit − UA) as a validation/warning on rent charges, (b) per-unit program tag(s) and set-aside %, (c) tenant-paid vs. subsidy portions when LIHTC is layered with Section 8/HCV.
- **Investor reporting** = configurable quarterly report pack (P&L vs. budget, balance sheet, occupancy, reserves, DSCR) + annual audit support. Deadlines are per partnership agreement, so templates/due dates must be configurable per entity.
- Asset management fees, partnership management fees, deferred developer fees, and cash-flow waterfalls (soft-loan "cash flow" payments) are entity-level accounting items — need multi-entity GL and payable/accrual tracking, not property-ops features.
- Most LIHTC deals are layered (LIHTC + HOME + HUD/RD); one property can carry multiple program rule sets, implying per-unit, multi-program tagging.

### Gaps
- No primary source pulled for state HFA annual owner certification content/deadlines (varies by state; typically via HFA online portals) or Form 8609 / 8586 mechanics.
- No primary source on standardized investor-report formats (none appears to exist; deal-specific).
- Did not verify HUD's annual LIHTC tenant data collection (HERA §2835) mechanics.

## 3. Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8 HCV) — landlord accounting perspective

### Takeaway
For a market-rate landlord accepting vouchers, the accounting need is narrow: split each tenant's rent charge into **tenant portion** and **HAP portion**, receive bulk HAP payments from the PHA and allocate them across tenant ledgers, and record **abatements** (daily pro-rated HAP withheld for failed inspections) — which the owner generally may not bill to the tenant.

### Cited Findings
- Abatement is the enforcement tool to ensure assisted units meet HQS/NSPIRE; the PHA cannot pay HAP for a failed unit. — [Mass. EOHLC HCV abatement memo](https://www.mass.gov/doc/hcvabatementmemo/download)
- Abatement is computed daily: monthly HAP ÷ days in month × days out of compliance. — [Mass. EOHLC memo](https://www.mass.gov/doc/hcvabatementmemo/download)
- HAP is not abated for tenant-caused violations. — [Mass. EOHLC memo](https://www.mass.gov/doc/hcvabatementmemo/download)
- Owners receive a repair window; abatement notice effective the first of the month after the cure period (per a local PHA plan). — [Prince William County HQS/rent reasonableness](https://www.pwcva.gov/assets/2026-08/08%20B%20Housing%20Quality%20Standards%20and%20Rent%20Reasonableness.pdf)
- Model HAP contract (HQS-era copy): PHA may not pay HAP on a failed unit unless the owner corrects within the PHA's period; PHA may not abate for HQS breaches the owner did not cause. — [South Bend copy of model HAP contract](https://docs.southbendin.gov/WebLink/0/doc/32542/Page7.aspx)
- NSPIRE for HCV was expected effective Oct 1, 2025, with HQS continuing if HUD delayed; a HUD notice extended the HCV NSPIRE compliance period (current status unverified). — [SHRA 2025 changes](https://www.shra.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2025-Mid-Year-Significant-Changes.pdf); [Montana NSPIRE chapter draft](https://commerce.mt.gov/_shared/housing/RentalHousing/docs/Amended-Chapter-8-NSPIRE-FINAL-DRAFT.pdf)

### Inferences
- Minimum feature set: (1) a "third-party payer" on a lease with a fixed monthly HAP amount and separate tenant amount; (2) a **bulk-receipt allocation** screen (one PHA deposit → many tenant ledgers, with remittance-detail import and unmatched/variance handling); (3) abatement adjustment entries (write-off of HAP portion, not shifted to tenant); (4) HAP change notices (retroactive adjustments when PHA changes the split mid-lease); (5) A/R aging separable by payer (tenant vs. PHA).
- Late fees and collections must not be applied to the HAP portion against the tenant — needs payer-aware late-fee logic.
- HCV is the lowest-effort, highest-reach affordable feature for a small-landlord product like this one (no HUD filing obligations on the landlord).

### Gaps
- Did not retrieve the current HUD model HAP contract (HUD-52641) text or the HCV tenancy addendum (HUD-52641-A) language on owner charges to tenant beyond rent-to-owner share.
- Did not verify how PHAs deliver remittance detail (formats vary by PHA; no standard found).

## 4. Other programs: USDA RD Section 515, HOME, state programs

### Takeaway
USDA RD Section 515 has its own budget/reporting cycle (Form RD 3560-7 project budget/utility allowance; Form RD 3560-10 balance sheet) governed by HB-2-3560/HB-3-3560 — a separate chart and format from HUD. HOME and state programs are layered rule sets with no single federal accounting format found.

### Cited Findings
- **Form RD 3560-7** = "Multiple Family Housing Project Budget/Utility Allowance"; detailed operating budget required in Section 515 loan packages; HFAs (e.g., WHEDA) require 3560-7 with typical proposed budget for tax-credit submittals on 515 projects. — [USDA RD 515 subsequent loans session (2023)](https://www.rd.usda.gov/sites/default/files/515-subsequent-loans-info-session.03.30.23.pdf); [WHEDA Appendix O](https://www.wheda.com/globalassets/documents/tax-credits/htc/2020/appendix-o-rd-submittal.pdf)
- **Form RD 3560-10** = "Multi Family Housing Balance Sheet" / "Borrower Balance Sheet" (listed in HB-3-3560 appendix). — [OMB ICR listing](https://omb.report/icr/201606-0575-001/ic/2050); [USDA HB-3-3560 Appendix 3](https://rd.usda.gov/files/3560-3appendix03.pdf)
- Governing handbooks: HB-2-3560 (asset management) and HB-3-3560 (servicing). — [HB-2-3560](https://WWW.RD.USDA.GOV/files/hb-2-3560.pdf); [HB-3-3560 consolidated](https://www.rd.usda.gov/media/file/download/usda-rd-hb-3-3560-consolidated.pdf)
- RealPage states OneSite Affordable HOTMA values for USDA RD went live July 1, 2025 (third-party summary of vendor claim) — implying RD has its own HOTMA track. — [ExactEstate comparison](https://www.exactestate.com/blog/best-affordable-housing-software-12-platforms-compared)

### Inferences
- RD 515 needs: annual budget in 3560-7 line format, year-end balance sheet in 3560-10 format, reserve account tracking, and Rental Assistance (RA) vs. tenant-paid split — conceptually similar to HUD but with different forms/line mapping. A "report mapping layer" (GL → program form lines) would serve HUD, RD, and investor formats from one ledger.
- HOME: compliance is mostly rent/income limits and period of affordability tracked by the participating jurisdiction; accounting impact is mainly soft-loan tracking and per-unit program tagging.

### Gaps
- Did not verify RD annual reporting deadlines, whether 3560-10 doubles as the annual financial report, or RD reserve/return-to-owner limits.
- No sources retrieved for HOME (24 CFR 92) owner reporting or state-program accounting requirements.
- USDA's MINC (Multi-Family Integrated System) electronic submission specifics not researched.

## 5. Fair Housing / data privacy relevant to financial data

### Takeaway
Tenant income/EIV data in HUD programs is Privacy Act–protected; owners must restrict access, keep signed Rules of Behavior, have written security procedures, and hold a current HUD-9887 consent before accessing EIV income data. Accounting systems that ingest subsidy/income data inherit these obligations.

### Cited Findings
- Privacy Act (5 USC 552a) requires HUD to account for everyone with access to systems containing PII; EIV data is Privacy Act–covered and misuse is penalized. — [HUD Form 90012 (EIV Rules of Behavior)](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/90012.pdf); [HUD-90011](https://hud.gov/sites/documents/90011.PDF)
- Owners/agents must develop and implement written policies on EIV data security and use; access limited to staff whose duties require it; signed Rules of Behavior kept in a Master EIV Access File. — [HUD Form 90012](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/90012.pdf); [Navigate Housing EIV resources](https://www.navigatehousing.com/resources/eiv/)
- A current **Form HUD-9887** consent must be on file before accessing employment/income data in EIV; Privacy Act bars disclosure without written consent. — [NAHMA analysis of Notice H 10-10](https://www.nahma.org/wp-content/uploads/files/member/NAHMAAnalysis/EIV%20Notice%20H10-10%20NAHMAnalysis.pdf)
- Reported penalties for misuse: felony, up to $5,000 fine, up to 5 years, plus civil damages (2010 source; verify). — [NAHMA News Sept/Oct 2010](https://www.nahma.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/NahmaNews_SeptOct10_FINAL.pdf)
- TRACS has its own Rules of Behavior for system users. — [HUD TRACS Rules of Behavior](https://hud.gov/sites/documents/TRACSROB.PDF)

### Inferences
- Product should: keep income/EIV documents out of the accounting module entirely (store only derived amounts: tenant rent, HAP); role-based access so maintenance/leasing staff can't see subsidy income detail; audit logs on access to tenant financial data; avoid exporting income data in general reports.
- Fair housing angle: source-of-income discrimination laws (state/local) mean voucher status should not drive screening or differential fees; a payer-aware ledger should not, e.g., auto-apply deposits/fees differently for voucher holders. (Not researched with sources — see gaps.)

### Gaps
- No sources pulled on state source-of-income protection laws, FCRA implications of tenant ledgers, or state data-breach laws as they apply to tenant financial data.

## 6. What affordable-capable PM systems provide vs. market-rate systems

### Takeaway
Affordable suites (Yardi Voyager Affordable, RealPage OneSite Affordable, plus MRI, Boston Post, ResMan) differentiate on **certification/compliance engines** (50059/TRACS, LIHTC TIC, RD, HOME, HOTMA updates), CA/TRACS transmission, and multi-program/multi-entity accounting — not on core GL. These are large, long-implementation products.

### Cited Findings
- Yardi Voyager Affordable: LIHTC, HUD (50059) contract compliance, HOME, USDA RD 515 and local programs on full multi-entity accounting. — [Yardi Voyager Affordable](https://www.yardi.com/product/voyager-affordable-housing/); [ExactEstate comparison (2026)](https://www.exactestate.com/blog/best-affordable-housing-software-12-platforms-compared)
- Yardi client case: management company moved off manual TRACS reporting processes to Voyager. — [Yardi AHEPA success story](https://www.yardi.com/about-us/success-stories/ahepa-management-company-2)
- Reviewer note: Yardi implementations are long and configuration typically needs paid expertise. — [ExactEstate comparison](https://www.exactestate.com/blog/best-affordable-housing-software-12-platforms-compared)
- RealPage states OneSite Affordable updated for **TRACS 2.0.3.A and HOTMA**; RD HOTMA values live July 1, 2025 (via third-party summary). — [ExactEstate comparison](https://www.exactestate.com/blog/best-affordable-housing-software-12-platforms-compared)
- RealPage historically: HUD subsidy + LIHTC in one UI; connectivity to contract administrators for real-time TRACS and special-claims exchange (2005 release). — [RealPage news](https://www.realpage.com/news/new-features-highlight-latest-version-of-realpages-on-demand-affordable-housing-software/)
- Yardi and RealPage participate in HUD's TRACS Working Group. — [HUD TRACS Working Group roster (2023)](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/Housing/documents/TRACS_Working_Group_Members-Registration_Report04142023.pdf)
- TRACS-compliant software generates the HUD-52670/52670A with the monthly submission. — [Navigate Housing](https://www.navigatehousing.com/avoid-tracs-submission-delays-before-voucher-processing/); [PHA-Web TRACS Certification Manager](https://pha-web.com/home/ContentPages/software/tenantManagement/tracs.aspx)

### Inferences
- Affordable-only features vs. market-rate (synthesized): certification workflows (move-in/annual/interim, TIC, 50059), income/rent limit tables by AMI, UA schedules, TRACS/MAT transmission and voucher generation, special claims, EIV handling, multi-program unit tagging, subsidy A/R by payer, HUD/RD chart-of-accounts mapping, restricted reserve/residual receipts accounts, surplus cash computation, investor reporting packs, and file audit (MOR/REAC/HFA) prep.
- **Recommended scope split for open-property** (aligned with README scope boundaries — no payment processing, no external portals):
  - *Support (low cost, broad value):* payer-split rent (tenant vs. HAP/subsidy), bulk subsidy receipt allocation, abatement entries, per-unit program tags, restricted cash/reserve sub-ledgers, configurable report mapping to HUD/RD/investor line items, CSV/trial-balance export for auditors.
  - *Maybe later:* surplus cash schedule (93486-style) and distribution guard; LIHTC rent-ceiling validation (limit − UA) as warnings.
  - *Explicitly out of scope:* TRACS/MAT transmission, 50059/TIC certification engines, EIV integration, FASS-MF/REAC submission, HOTMA income calculations, state HFA portal reporting. These are regulated, versioned, and the core moat of Yardi/RealPage; integrate or import instead.

### Gaps
- No primary documentation retrieved for MRI, Boston Post, or ResMan affordable modules.
- No independent, current feature-by-feature comparison; HUD does not publish a certified-vendor list found in this research.
