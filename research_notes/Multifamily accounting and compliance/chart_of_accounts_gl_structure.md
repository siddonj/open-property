# Multifamily Chart of Accounts (COA) and General Ledger (GL) Structure — US, 2026

Scope note: Sources split into (a) **authoritative / regulatory**: the HUD Handbook 4370.2 prescribed chart and its state housing finance agency (HFA) derivatives (MHDC, WHEDA), plus Fannie Mae's Multifamily Selling & Servicing Guide (lender underwriting definitions); and (b) **vendor convention**: Buildium, Rentec Direct, Yardi Kube, Yardi Voyager/MRI consultant descriptions. **No industry-wide (NAA/NMHC/IREM/NCREIF/NAREIT) mandated multifamily COA was found.** The HUD chart is the only published, numbered, account-by-account multifamily standard I could verify.

## 1. Standard / widely used multifamily COAs and their numbering schemes

### Takeaway
The HUD multifamily chart (Handbook 4370.2, Ch. 4; carried forward by HUD FASS-MF and state HFAs) is the de facto published standard: 1xxx assets, 2xxx liabilities, 3xxx equity, 5xxx revenue (incl. vacancy contra-revenue at 52xx), 6xxx operating expenses, 7xxx non-operating/entity items. Commercial PM software (Buildium, Rentec, Yardi Kube) uses a generic 1000/2000/3000/4000/5000+ scheme with revenue in 4xxx; there is no single vendor-neutral standard.

### Cited Findings
**HUD (authoritative for HUD-insured/assisted projects)**
- HUD 4370.2 ¶2-4 "Prescribed Accounts": prescribed accounts "must be maintained as outlined and described in Chapter 4"; no project uses all accounts; any needed accounts "should be placed in the groupings and order shown in the Chart of Accounts, Chapter 4"; additional accounts may be added per GAAP. Consistency exists so HUD can ingest statements "without any interpretation of the accounts." — [HUD Handbook 4370.2 REV-1, Ch. 2](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43702c2hsgh.pdf)
- HUD 4370.2 ¶2-3: books of original entry kept current; postings to ledger "at least monthly"; standard journal entries for recurring items allowed; keep operating funds separate from other project funds (esp. construction vs operating). — [HUD 4370.2 Ch. 2](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43702c2hsgh.pdf)
- Older 4370.2 numbering: 1100 Current Assets — 1110 Petty Cash, 1120 Cash in Bank, 1130 Tenant/Member A/R; 5100 Rent Revenue – Gross Potential — 5120 Rent Revenue – Apartments. FASS-era revision relabeled 1120 as "Cash – Operations" (consolidated unrestricted cash). — [search summary of HUD 4370.2/4370.3 and FASS appendices](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43703c6hsgh.pdf); [HUD FASS-MF archive](https://www.hud.gov/reac/fass-fha-archive)

**MHDC (Missouri HFA) chart, revised 12/22/2016 — a full FASS-style HUD-derived chart with definitions** (all items below from [MHDC Chart of Accounts](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf); `*` = required field):

**Vendor conventions (not standards)**
- Buildium recommends ranges of 1,000: Assets 1000–1999, Liabilities 2000–2999, Equity 3000–3999, Revenue 4000–4999, Expenses 5000–5999; ships a standard rental-property COA that users can add/edit/deactivate; can record transactions at unit level. Its blog example encodes the property in the last digit of child accounts (e.g., 1101/1201 for 123 Elm St.). — [Buildium blog](https://www.buildium.com/blog/chart-of-accounts-property-management)
- Rentec Direct: Assets 1000–1999, Liabilities 2000–2999, Equity 3000–3999, Income 4000–4999, Expenses 5000–8999; defaults include "Security Deposits Held" and "Prepaid Rents" (liabilities), "Retained Earnings" (system-calculated, not postable), "Owner Investment", "Opening Balance"; income: Rental Income, Tenant-Reimbursed Expense, HOA Dues, CAM Income; property asset accounts created as sub-accounts of "Real Estate". — [Rentec Direct help](https://help.rentecdirect.com/article/843-chart-of-accounts)
- Yardi Kube default list (per search snippet; page itself returned 401): 2200 Accounts Payable, 2250 Security Deposits, 2210 prepay liability for accrual accounting, 4520 Prepaid Rent (shown as an income-statement account). — [Yardi Kube help (snippet)](https://help.yardikube.com/en/articles/12013694-default-gl-accounts)
- MRI advises multifamily users to track employee rent concessions, resident concessions, and prior- vs current-month vacancy accounts. — [MRI via search summary](https://www.mrisoftware.com/nz/?p=61788)

### Inferences
- For a small/mid-size app, the cleanest approach is a **4-digit HUD-shaped chart** (5xxx revenue with 52xx contra-revenue, 6xxx opex grouped 62/63/64/65/67/68, 7xxx non-operating), since it is the only published, defined standard and maps directly to HUD/HFA reporting for affordable properties; alternatively offer a "conventional" template in the Buildium/Rentec style (4xxx revenue) and keep a mapping layer to HUD codes.
- Buildium's "property in last digit" pattern is an anti-pattern for a multi-property app (see §5); prefer a property dimension.
- The "*required" flags in MHDC suggest a minimal required set an app should seed and prevent deletion of: Cash–Operations, Tenant Deposits Held in Trust (asset + liability), Accrued Interest–1st Mortgage, Mortgage Payable–1st, Total Equity, GPR, Vacancies–Apartments, Mgmt Fee, Audit, RE Taxes, Property Insurance, Mortgage Interest.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve HUD 4370.2 **Chapter 4** (the actual prescribed chart) or the current FASS-MF chart; MHDC's chart is used as the closest verified proxy. Numbering of older 4370.2 differs in places (e.g., 1120 "Cash in Bank" vs "Cash – Operations").
- No NAA, NMHC, IREM, NCREIF, or NAREIT published account-numbered COA was found. NAREIT/NCREIF focus on fund/REIT reporting metrics, not property COAs (not verified in this session).
- AppFolio, RealPage, Entrata, ResMan, Rent Manager default chart numbers not verifiable from public sources (help centers behind login).

## 2. Standard revenue accounts and economic occupancy

### Takeaway
Multifamily revenue is booked as **Gross Potential Rent less contra-revenue lines** (loss/gain-to-lease, vacancy, concessions, non-revenue units, bad debt) to arrive at net rental income, then other income is added to reach EGI. Economic occupancy ≈ net rental collections ÷ GPR; it is always ≤ physical occupancy when concessions, loss-to-lease or bad debt exist.

### Cited Findings
- HUD/HFA structure: GPR (5120) + tenant assistance (5121) = total GPR; contra accounts 5220 Vacancies, 5250 Concessions, 5260 Loss to Lease, 5270 Parking vacancy, 5290 Misc vacancy; other income 5910 Laundry/Vending, 5920 Tenant Charges (NSF, late, lease break, forfeited deposits, damages), 5954 Cable/Internet, 5170 Parking, 5490 interest. — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- HUD/HFA treats **non-revenue units as expenses, not contra-revenue**: 6312 Office or Model Apartment Rent, 6331 Administrative Rent-Free Unit, 6521 O&M Rent-Free Unit, 6531 Security Rent-Free Unit — i.e., GPR stays at 100% and the unit's rent is expensed in the department of the employee/use. — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- HUD/HFA books **bad debt as an admin expense** (6370 Bad Debts), not contra-revenue. — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- Fannie Mae underwriting (Guide effective 10/26/2026; table shown is the manufactured-housing variant of the Underwritten NCF table, which follows the same structure): Gross Rental Income + non-revenue units (model unit rent deducted as G&A "model apartment" expense; employee unit rent deducted in "payroll and benefits") = **GPR**; minus **physical vacancy**; minus **concessions** ("forgone residential rental income from incentives granted to tenants for signing leases, such as free rent for 1 or more months, move-in allowance"); minus **bad debt** ("unpaid rental income determined to be uncollectable, including any adjustments to other income for bad debt") = **Net Rental Income**; plus other income (stable, common in market, excluding one-time items; laundry, vending, parking, all other) = **EGI**. — [Fannie Mae Multifamily Guide Part III §606](https://mfguide.fanniemae.com/fnmf-pdf/download/3996)
- Fannie Mae Glossary: EGI = Net Rental Income + commercial income + other income; NOI = EGI minus operating expenses. — [Fannie Mae Guide glossary](https://mfguide.fanniemae.com/fnmf-pdf/download/3996)
- IREM/NAA Income/Expense IQ benchmark income analysis fields: gross rents, **loss/gain to lease**, gross potential rent, rent adjustments, net effective rent, other revenue; total gross rents include rents, retail, parking, other (2024 national multifamily: $20,575/unit total gross rents; rents $20,369, retail $21.79, parking $176.50, other $7.98). — [IREM Income/Expense IQ National Summary 2024](https://www.irem.org/file%20library/globalnavigation/learning/tools/irem-income-expense-iq-national-summary-24.pdf)
- Loss to lease = rent forgone on occupied units whose in-place leases are below market; modeled as a separate line from vacancy between GPR and EGI. — [Adventures in CRE / search summary](https://www.adventuresincre.com/the-road-to-a-stabilized-noi-concessions-in-real-estate-underwriting/); [MRI Software](https://www.mrisoftware.com/?p=90196)
- MHDC defines loss-to-lease narrowly as timing (rent increase effective 1/1 vs. lease expiring 4/1 = 3 months lost to lease). — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- Economic occupancy = Collected Revenue ÷ Gross Potential Rent × 100 (vendor glossary); economic vacancy bundles vacancy, concessions, down units, delinquency and runs above physical vacancy. — [HelloData](https://www.hellodata.ai/help-articles/how-to-calculate-economic-occupancy); [Cove glossary](https://cove.is/glossary/economic-vacancy). Vendor rule of thumb of "90%+ is good" is not a standard. — [Rentana](https://www.rentana.io/glossary/economic-occupancy)
- Some agency/assessor surveys keep loss-to-lease out of the vacancy section. — [Fairfax County Apartment Survey](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/taxes/sites/taxes/files/Assets/Documents/PDF/real-estate/Apartment-Survey.pdf)
- RUBS/utility reimbursement: Rentec ships a "Tenant-Reimbursed Expense" income account; Fannie counts it within "all other income" subject to stability tests. — [Rentec Direct](https://help.rentecdirect.com/article/843-chart-of-accounts); [Fannie Mae Guide](https://mfguide.fanniemae.com/fnmf-pdf/download/3996)

### Inferences
- Recommended revenue block for the app (inference, HUD-compatible):
  - 5100 GPR – Market Rent (scheduled market rent × all units, incl. vacant/model/employee)
  - 5105 Gain/(Loss) to Lease (market minus lease rent on occupied units; debit = loss)
  - 5120 GPR at lease (= 5100 ± 5105, or post lease rent directly and skip market-based GPR if no market-rent data)
  - 5220 Vacancy Loss; 5250 Concessions; 5255 Non-Revenue Units (Model/Employee/Admin) **or** HUD-style expense accounts 6312/6331/6521; 5280 Bad Debt / Write-offs (or 6370 if following HUD); 5285 Bad Debt Recoveries
  - 59xx Other income: Late/NSF fees, Pet rent/fees, Parking, Utility reimbursement/RUBS, Application fees, Damages/cleaning, Lease-break fees, Laundry/vending, Interest
- Economic occupancy formula the app can compute directly from GL: (GPR − vacancy − concessions − non-revenue − bad debt) ÷ GPR, or more simply rent collected ÷ GPR. Both conventions exist; the app should label which one it uses. Including loss-to-lease in the denominator gap is a choice (market-based GPR) — make it explicit.
- Since the app already tracks units, leases and rent charges, GPR/vacancy/loss-to-lease postings can be generated from the rent roll each period rather than entered manually (the "potential rent" posting method). This requires a market-rent field per unit.

### Gaps
- No authoritative multifamily standard (NAA/IREM) found defining loss-to-lease vs. gain-to-lease account treatment or whether bad debt is contra-revenue vs. expense; practice differs (HUD = expense; Fannie = deduction from rental income).
- Exact IREM/NAA I/E IQ GL mapping definitions not public; IREM states I/E IQ is being discontinued after 12/31/2025 (per search summary of IREM page — not independently fetched). — [IREM I/E IQ page](https://www.irem.org/learning/tools/income-expense-iq)

## 3. Expense categories, "below the line" items, and NOI

### Takeaway
Standard operating categories: administrative/G&A, marketing/leasing, payroll & benefits, repairs & maintenance (incl. contract services and make-ready/turnover), utilities, management fee, real estate taxes, insurance. NOI = EGI − operating expenses. Excluded from NOI ("below the line"): debt service (interest and principal), depreciation/amortization, capital expenditures and replacement-reserve deposits, owner/entity costs (asset management, partnership, incentive fees, entity legal, income taxes).

### Cited Findings
- HUD/HFA operating groups: Administrative 62xx–63xx (incl. advertising 6210, management fee 6320, legal 6340, audit 6350, bad debt 6370); Utilities 64xx; Operating & Maintenance 65xx (payroll, supplies, contracts, trash, security, HVAC, snow, pool, exterminating, elevator, **6580 Vacant Unit Preparation** = turnover); Taxes & Insurance 67xx (RE taxes, payroll taxes, property insurance, workers' comp, health/benefits); Financial 68xx (mortgage interest, MIP); Depreciation/Amortization 6600/6610; Non-operating 71xx (officer salaries, **7115 Asset Mgmt, Partnership & Incentive Fees** payable only from surplus cash, entity legal 7120, income taxes 7130, subordinate/partner loan interest). — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- HUD places mortgage interest (68xx) and depreciation (66xx) inside the 6xxx income statement, but principal and replacement-reserve deposits are memo "other cash items" (7001/7002), and transfers from reserves that were expensed are tracked in 7003. — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- HUD Replacement Reserve "provides cash for the replacement of capital items" (HVAC, re-plumbing, roofing, sprinklers) and "is not intended to pay for routine maintenance costs"; funded by a lump sum at closing plus monthly deposits with the mortgage payment; withdrawals require written request with invoices. — [HUD 4370.2 ¶2-7](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43702c2hsgh.pdf)
- HUD surplus cash = cash remaining after all mortgage payments, required reserve deposits, and project obligations, and after segregating special funds and **all tenant security deposits held**; computed annually (Form HUD-93486). — [HUD 4370.2 ¶2-8](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43702c2hsgh.pdf)
- Fannie Mae Underwritten NCF: EGI − line-by-line stabilized opex (non-recurring/extraordinary excluded) − property management fee (greatest of 3% of EGI, actual, or appraiser's market fee; subordinated non-arm's-length portion excluded) − real estate taxes − insurance − "utilities, water and sewer, repairs and maintenance, payroll and benefits, advertising and marketing, professional fees, general and administrative, ground rent, and all other expenses" = **Underwritten NOI**; − Replacement Reserve = **Underwritten NCF**. — [Fannie Mae Guide §606](https://mfguide.fanniemae.com/fnmf-pdf/download/3996)
- IREM/NAA I/E IQ operating expense groupings: administrative, management fees, leasing expenses, repairs and maintenance, utilities, taxes, insurance, other → NOI. — [IREM 2024 National Summary](https://www.irem.org/file%20library/globalnavigation/learning/tools/irem-income-expense-iq-national-summary-24.pdf)
- Replacement reserves: conventional definition excludes them from NOI; many lenders deduct them before NOI for conservatism; cash flow is the same either way. — [Wall Street Prep](https://www.wallstreetprep.com/knowledge/replacement-reserves)
- CMBS servicers use the CREFC Operating Statement Analysis Report and NOI Adjustment Worksheet to "normalize" NOI/DSCR; NOI/NCF may follow loan-document definitions. — [SEC filing, WFRBS 2014-C25 PSA](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001623838/000091412116000975/rb34549837-ex99_1.htm)

### Inferences
- Recommended P&L layout for the app (inference): Revenue (5xxx) → **EGI** → Controllable opex (payroll, R&M, make-ready/turnover, contract services, marketing, admin, utilities) → Non-controllable opex (management fee, RE taxes, insurance) → **NOI** → below-the-line section (8xxx/9xxx or HUD 68xx/66xx/71xx): mortgage interest, depreciation, amortization, asset-mgmt/partnership fees, entity legal/income tax, capex, replacement-reserve deposits, principal → Cash flow after debt service.
- Flag each expense account with `is_operating` (in NOI) and `is_controllable` booleans rather than relying on number ranges, so the NOI report is robust to custom accounts.
- Capex: book to balance-sheet fixed asset accounts (14xx) and depreciate; paying from reserves debits the asset and credits 1320 Replacement Reserve — no P&L hit (consistent with HUD's 7003 "transfers from reserves" memo when an expensed item was reimbursed).

### Gaps
- CREFC Operating Statement template and IRP line definitions not retrieved (behind CREFC membership).
- No authoritative NAA/IREM definition distinguishing "make-ready/turnover" from "repairs & maintenance" beyond HUD's 6580 definition.

## 4. Multi-entity / multi-property structures (owner vs. management company books, consolidation, intercompany)

### Takeaway
Each property (or owning LLC) is normally its own accounting entity with its own balanced books; the management company keeps separate books; trust/fiduciary money (rents, deposits) is tracked per owner/property and reconciled three ways (bank, ledger, owner balances). HUD permits pooled centralized operating cash only if each project's position is identifiable at all times — the GL pattern for that is a due-to/due-from (intercompany) per property against the pooled bank.

### Cited Findings
- Yardi and MRI "both treat each property or fund as its own accounting entity"; Yardi uses property, entity, account, and department as accounting dimensions; MRI postings are root account + class/branch with entity and department as separate fields; LLCs/JVs/funds introduce intercompany accounts and consolidations. — [REdirect Consulting](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/gl-coding-in-real-estate-how-the-general-ledger-and-chart-of-accounts-work-in-yardi-and-mri)
- Yardi Voyager supports book and entity hierarchies that roll property statements into consolidated entity reports without double-counting, if designed in from the start. — [JMCO](https://www.jmco.com/articles/real-estate/why-your-yardi-chart-of-accounts-is-costing-you-time-every-month-and-how-to-fix-it/)
- HUD 4370.2 ¶2-6: management agents may hold project funds in a **centralized account** if (among other conditions) "Deposits to and disbursements from the centralized account must clearly be traceable to each project. The actual cash position of each and every project in the centralized account must be easily identifiable at all times"; owners may opt out; separate subsidiary payroll accounts allowed; Section 8 receipts may go directly to the centralized account if identified by project. — [HUD 4370.2 ¶2-6](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43702c2hsgh.pdf)
- HUD minimum bank accounts per project: Regular Operating, Replacement Reserve (held by mortgagee), Tenant Security Deposit (if deposits collected), Residual Receipts. — [HUD 4370.2 ¶2-5](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43702c2hsgh.pdf)
- HUD/HFA chart separates project vs. owner-entity items with paired accounts: 1120 Cash–Operations vs 1125 Cash–Entity; 1140 vs 1145 receivables; 1510 vs 1515 investments; 2113/2305 A/P–Entity (payable from surplus cash); 7105 Entity Revenue; 7115 partnership/asset mgmt fees. — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- Distributions: any withdrawal of project cash/assets other than for reasonable operating expenses is a distribution, governed by the regulatory agreement (profit-motivated: all surplus cash distributable; non-profit: to residual receipts; limited dividend: capped). — [HUD 4370.2 ¶2-8, 2-10](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43702c2hsgh.pdf)
- PM trust accounting practice: operating-company money (management fees, business expenses) kept separate from trust/owner money (rents, owner distributions). — [Buildium bookkeeping guide](https://www.buildium.com/blog/rental-property-bookkeeping-setup-tips/)
- Each property or owner has an individual ledger within trust accounting; monthly **three-way reconciliation** (bank = ledger = sum of owner balances). — [Rentvine](https://www.rentvine.com/blog/what-is-trust-accounting-property-management); per-owner virtual trust ledgers — [Hemlane](https://hemlane.com/resources/quickbooks-trust-accounting-property-management/)
- State regulators require property/owner ledgers in addition to tenant ledgers (NC Rule A .0117(c)(4)). — [NC Real Estate Commission bulletin](https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/multiple-trust-account-violations-a-regulatory-affairs-division-case-study/)
- Buildium: equity accounts carry "property contributions and distributions" (owner contributions/draws). — [Buildium blog](https://www.buildium.com/blog/chart-of-accounts-property-management)

### Inferences
- Data model for the app (inference):
  - `entity` (owner LLC / management company) → owns 1..n `property`. Every journal line carries `entity_id` + `property_id`; balancing is enforced **per entity** (and ideally per property, since owners expect property-level balance sheets).
  - Owner books per property: owner equity accounts 3100 Owner Contributions, 3200 Owner Distributions, 3900 Retained Earnings (system-closed). Management-company books (separate entity): 4xxx Management Fee Income, Leasing Fees, etc.
  - If one bank account is pooled across properties (allowed by HUD only with per-project traceability; common in small PM shops' trust accounts), post cash to the pooled bank account in a "trust" entity and record per-property **1150 Due From Trust / 2150 Due To Property (or Due To Owner)**; reconciliation report = bank balance vs. sum of property due-to balances vs. owner ledger balances (three-way).
  - Management fee: expense 6320 on the property books; income on the management company books; settled through Due To Management Co. (2125) until paid. Intercompany pairs must net to zero on consolidation; consolidation report = sum of entities with intercompany accounts eliminated.
- Simplest viable path for a small/mid app: single-tenant "one company manages N properties", one property = one set of books (property_id as the balancing dimension), with an optional `owner` grouping for consolidated reporting. Defer full multi-entity intercompany until a customer has pooled bank accounts.

### Gaps
- No authoritative (GAAP/AICPA) source retrieved on consolidation of property LLCs specifically for multifamily; no vendor docs retrieved on AppFolio/Buildium internal due-to/due-from mechanics.
- State-by-state trust account rules for property managers not covered here (separate research area).

## 5. Dimensions / segments: in the account number vs. separate tags

### Takeaway
Modern practice: keep the natural account (e.g., 4-digit) clean and carry property, entity, unit, building, department, and job/project as **separate dimensions/segments**, not encoded into account numbers. Encoding property into the account number causes account explosion and breaks cross-property comparison.

### Cited Findings
- A well-built Yardi COA "uses segments and dimensions to separate property-level activity from entity-level activity without multiplying account volume"; a property dimension lets the same account codes be compared across properties; use one consistent code per expense type across properties (bad example: landscaping in 6100 at one property, 6150 at another). — [JMCO](https://www.jmco.com/articles/real-estate/why-your-yardi-chart-of-accounts-is-costing-you-time-every-month-and-how-to-fix-it/)
- Real estate ERPs support user-defined segments (department, location, deal, unit) to avoid new accounts; poor segment design yields "thousands of near-duplicate accounts"; real estate charts often carry recoverable vs non-recoverable versions of expense accounts (commercial CAM). — [REdirect Consulting](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/gl-coding-in-real-estate-how-the-general-ledger-and-chart-of-accounts-work-in-yardi-and-mri)
- Counter-example (vendor blog): Buildium suggests property codes in the last digit of child accounts (1101, 1201 for 123 Elm St.) and per-building/per-unit expense accounts for larger multifamily. — [Buildium blog](https://www.buildium.com/blog/chart-of-accounts-property-management); [Buildium PM COA blog](https://buildium.com/blog/property-management-chart-of-accounts)
- Buildium's product records transactions at the unit level (i.e., unit as a transaction attribute). — [Buildium blog](https://www.buildium.com/blog/chart-of-accounts-property-management)
- "Build the chart backward from reporting needs": detail accounts can be rolled up, but a blended account cannot be split later. — [REdirect Consulting](https://www.redirectconsulting.com/blog/gl-coding-in-real-estate-how-the-general-ledger-and-chart-of-accounts-work-in-yardi-and-mri)

### Inferences
- Journal line schema for the app: `account_id` (natural account) + `property_id` (required) + `unit_id` (nullable) + `building_id` (nullable, derivable from unit) + `vendor_id`/`tenant_id`/`lease_id` (subledger link, nullable) + `work_order_id`/`project_id` (nullable; for capex/turn jobs). Department is optional; HUD already splits payroll by department via separate accounts (6310 office, 6330 manager, 6510 maintenance, 6530 security).
- Use **report trees / account groups** (e.g., `account.group` = "Utilities", `account.report_line` = "Controllable Opex") instead of numeric-range logic to produce P&L/NOI and HUD-mapping outputs. A per-account `hud_code` mapping column lets one chart produce both conventional and HUD-format statements.
- Existing app entities (properties, units, tenants, leases, vendors, maintenance work orders, recurring tasks) map naturally onto these dimensions; maintenance spend can post with `work_order_id` to support make-ready vs. R&M reporting.

### Gaps
- No public documentation of Yardi Voyager/AppFolio internal GL field structure (e.g., Yardi's "account tree" format or digit counts) was retrievable.

## 6. Multifamily-specific balance sheet accounts

### Takeaway
Core multifamily balance sheet: operating cash; security-deposit cash held in trust (separate bank account) with an offsetting tenant security-deposit liability; tenant A/R with allowance; prepaid rent (liability); escrows (taxes/insurance/MIP) and replacement/operating reserves held by lender; fixed assets with accumulated depreciation; accrued interest, current and long-term mortgage payable, debt issuance costs (contra-liability).

### Cited Findings
- **Operating cash**: 1120 Cash – Operations (all unrestricted operating cash incl. petty cash); 2105 Bank Overdraft – Operations used when negative. — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- **Security deposit cash**: 1191 Tenant Deposits Held in Trust — must be held "in the name of the projects in a separate bank account"; may be used for tenant damages and delinquent rent when a tenant vacates. **Liability**: 2191 Tenant Deposits Held in Trust (Contra). — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- HUD: one Security Deposit Account per project; funds "must not be commingled with any other funds" (other projects, operating, managing agent); disbursements only for refunds and appropriate tenant-incurred expenses; state law also governs. — [HUD 4370.2 ¶2-9](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/43702c2hsgh.pdf)
- Vendor naming: Rentec "Security Deposits Held" (liability); Buildium lists refundable security deposits as a liability; Yardi Kube 2250 Security Deposits. Deposits stay off the P&L until refunded or applied to charges. — [Rentec Direct](https://help.rentecdirect.com/article/843-chart-of-accounts); [Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/blog/chart-of-accounts-property-management); [Yardi Kube (snippet)](https://help.yardikube.com/en/articles/12013694-default-gl-accounts)
- **Forfeited deposits** become income (5920 Tenant Charges). — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- **Prepaid rent**: 2210 Prepaid Revenue (rents for future periods; also enters surplus-cash computation). Rentec "Prepaid Rents" liability; Yardi Kube 2210 prepay liability for accrual. Tax note: advance rent is taxable in the year received (cash-basis tax vs. GAAP accrual differ). — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf); [Rentec Direct](https://help.rentecdirect.com/article/843-chart-of-accounts); [Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/blog/chart-of-accounts-property-management)
- **Tenant receivables**: 1130 Tenant A/R; 1131 Allowance for Doubtful Accounts; 1135 A/R – HUD (subsidy). — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- **Escrows/reserves**: 1310 Escrow Deposits (insurance, RE taxes, mortgage insurance premiums); 1320 Replacement Reserve; 1330 Other Reserves (operating, lease-up); 1340 Residual Receipts. — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- **Debt**: 2131 Accrued Interest Payable – First Mortgage (accrue interest included in next month's payment at period end); 2170 Mortgage Payable – current portion; 2320 Mortgage Payable – long-term net of current portion; 2340 Debt Issuance Costs (presented as reduction of LT liabilities; amortized through 6822). — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- **Accrued operating liabilities**: 2120 Accrued Wages, 2121 Accrued Payroll Taxes, 2123 Accrued Management Fee, 2150 Accrued Property Taxes. — [MHDC](https://mhdc.com/media/3illlhjy/mhdc-chart-of-accounts.pdf)
- AppFolio's security deposit funds detail report can serve as a sub-ledger for deposit liability GL accounts. — [Proper.ai](https://www.proper.ai/blog/appfolio-tips-from-expert-property-accountants)

### Inferences
- Suggested seeded balance-sheet accounts (inference): 1010 Operating Cash; 1020 Security Deposit Trust Cash; 1030 Petty Cash; 1050 Undeposited Funds; 1130 Tenant A/R (control account, subledger = tenant/lease); 1131 Allowance for Doubtful Accounts; 1150 Due From Trust/Owner; 1200 Prepaid Expenses; 1310 Tax & Insurance Escrow; 1320 Replacement Reserve; 1330 Operating Reserve; 14xx Land/Buildings/Improvements/Equipment; 1495 Accumulated Depreciation; 2010 Accounts Payable (control, subledger = vendor); 2120 Accrued Liabilities; 2131 Accrued Interest; 2150 Due To Owner/Management Co.; 2191 Tenant Security Deposits Held (subledger = lease); 2192 Pet Deposits Held (if tracked separately); 2210 Prepaid Rent; 2170/2320 Mortgage Payable current/LT; 2340 Debt Issuance Costs; 3100 Owner Contributions; 3200 Owner Distributions; 3900 Retained Earnings.
- Enforce invariants in code: (a) security-deposit liability balance per property = sum of deposits on active/unsettled leases (subledger tie-out); (b) deposit trust cash ≥ deposit liability (HUD segregation requirement); (c) A/R control = sum of tenant ledgers; (d) prepaid rent auto-applies to next period's charges.
- Cash vs. accrual: small owners often report cash-basis; supporting both implies posting accrual entries (charges → A/R) and deriving cash-basis reports by filtering to cash-touching transactions, or maintaining two "books" (Yardi's book concept). The latter is out of scope for an MVP.

### Gaps

- Pet deposits, last-month's-rent deposits, and interest-bearing deposit requirements vary by state; not covered here.
